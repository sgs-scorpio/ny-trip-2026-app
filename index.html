What to Pack

  • Beach tent or umbrella (shade for Viv!)
  • Large beach blanket or mat
  • Beach toys (buckets, shovels, molds)
  • Cooler with snacks & drinks
  • LOTS of sunscreen (SPF 50+, water-resistant)
  • Hats & sunglasses for everyone
  • Beach towels (one per person + extras)
  • Swim diapers for Viv
  • Life jackets if needed
  • Water shoes (optional, sand can get hot)
  • Baby powder (helps remove sand from skin)
  • Plastic bags for wet items

  • Swimsuits (wear under clothes)
  • Rash guards for kids (sun protection)
  • Complete change of clothes for car ride home
  • Extra outfit for Viv (accidents)

  • Water bottles (keep hydrated!)
  • Juice boxes
  • Easy snacks (goldfish, pretzels, fruit, sandwiches)
  • Ice packs for cooler
🩹 Other:
  • First aid kit (band-aids, aloe)
  • Diapers & wipes
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Tissues
  • Portable phone charger
  • Cash for parking/concessions

Important Beach Safety

  • 🏊 Always stay near lifeguard stations
  • ☀️ Apply sunscreen 30 min before sun exposure, reapply every 2 hours
  • 👀 NEVER take eyes off kids near water - not even for a second
  • 🌊 Check flag conditions (green=calm, yellow=moderate, red=dangerous)
  • 💧 Keep kids hydrated - offer water every 30 min
  • 🏖️ Set up umbrella/tent before kids in sun
  • ⏰ Leave BEFORE anyone gets overtired or sunburned
  • 🚿 Use outdoor showers to rinse sand before car

Why Morning Beach Sessions Work Best

  • ✅ Cooler temperatures (not blazing hot yet)
  • ✅ Less crowded (easier parking, more space)
  • ✅ Protects nap schedule (home by noon)
  • ✅ Kids have morning energy
  • ✅ Still have afternoon pool time at home!
  • ✅ Less sun exposure overall (safer)
  • ✅ Can do 2-3 beach days without exhaustion

Weather Considerations

Perfect beach day: Sunny, 75-85°F, light breeze

Skip beach if:
  • Rain or thunderstorms
  • High wind (sand blowing, rough waves)
  • Extreme heat (95°F+)
  • Red flag conditions
Backup: Pool day at home instead!

Frequency Guide

Recommended: 2-3 beach visits over 2 weeks

Why not more?
  • Beach days are exhausting (sun, heat, logistics)
  • You have pool at home (easier water fun)
  • 2-3 times is special, 5+ times gets routine
  • Leaves energy for NYC trips & family time
When to go:
  • Week 1: Once or twice (early in trip)
  • Week 2: Once (later in trip)
  • Check weather forecast 2-3 days ahead
